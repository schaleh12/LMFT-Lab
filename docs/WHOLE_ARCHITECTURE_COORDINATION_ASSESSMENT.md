# Whole-architecture coordination: assessment and proposed next increment

2026-10-13. First task of the newly authorized session, 01:25–05:25 UTC. Assessment
only; the proposal below is not an approved or executed experiment. Existing work
and accepted negative findings remain intact. No model calls, Jev, embodiment,
seed/numerical changes or new runtime mode.

**Finding:** the repository has useful persistent evidence mechanisms, but does not
yet implement the intended four-domain coordination. A result from its small
authored circuit cannot settle the whole architectural hypothesis. Conversely,
activating all nodes would not establish coordination or usefulness.

**Original recommendation (superseded by the review addendum below):** an isolated persistent **interpretation-to-action handoff**
on the existing calibrated sensor bench, with authority, history and delayed
consequences recorded together. This tests a real missing connection without
inventing an audit trigger or new seed meanings. It is an engineering integration
gate, not a claim to have operationalized the complete founding architecture.

## 1. Authority and intended roles

[Minimal Specification §4](MINIMAL_LMFT_SPEC.md), [concept](LMFT_CONCEPT.md) and
[runtime specification](LMFT_RUNTIME_SPEC.md) distinguish the fixed 40-node genealogy
from learned associations, numerical rules and interpretations. `birth.py` contains
the canonical four roots, twelve distinctions and twenty-four leaves. The proposed
full experience cycle includes appraisal, memory, propagation, contemplation,
evaluation and persisted consequences; individual fixture modes implement subsets.

The owner's current framing is recorded as intent, not retroactive implementation:

| Intended role | Present machinery that could contribute | What is not established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cultural: external context | Scoped permissions, declared task constraints, source/provenance and work budgets exist outside the seed circuit | Freedom conditioning Justice and both conditioning Efficiency have no agreed executable semantics; no institution/nationality reliability weights |
| Experiential: internal experience | Evidence journals, correction lineage, remembered calibration and restart; some authored attention inputs | Reflection conditioning Phase and both conditioning Synchronization is unimplemented; novelty/salience are not a complete appraisal of personal experience |
| Philosophical: contextual/metacognitive interpretation | Explicit assumptions, alternative hypotheses, scope and interpretation versions can be inspected | Being/Structure/Persistence do not presently select interpretations or actions; Agency/Existence, Order/Chaos and Continuity/Discreteness are not fixed dictionary codes |
| Psychological: coordination | Question selection, support gates, requests, abstention and budget bookkeeping | Contemplation does not yet coordinate all three other domains, reconcile their conflicts or perform open-ended endogenous deliberation |

Deferral is an explicit architectural state, not missing decoration. The abstract
Being/Structure/Persistence distinctions need sufficiently stable context resolution,
contrasting observations and a demonstrated decision consequence before an operational
interpretation is selected. Phase/Synchronization need temporally grounded experience
and a task-defined account of consequences before affect-like numerical meanings
are justified. Cultural dependencies need explicit scoped constraints and observable
tradeoffs before any normative association controls behavior. These prerequisites
can develop incrementally; no complete philosophical dictionary is required now.

Any prospective cross-domain association should remain a versioned, sourced hypothesis
with its scope, uncertainty, counterexamples and rollback recorded. An illustrative
connection or descriptive label is not accepted decision authority. Promotion to a
trusted rule needs separately frozen evidence/acceptance criteria and comparison with
a simpler direct mechanism. The next increment below deliberately leaves these
abstract roles deferred while testing an already evidenced interpretation mechanism.

These potential correspondences do not make an ordinary permission check a learned
concept of Justice, or a timestamp an implementation of Continuity. The stated
three 3×2 structures feeding Psychological control remain a research proposal.
Do not silently translate their dependencies into multiplication, priority order,
new graph edges or moral scoring. All paired seed meanings need not be opposites.

## 2. One actual decision: r8/c9, correction followed by a wrong answer

This existing case is available in the shared action runtime (r1/r8/r2), independently
of the retired terminal wrapper. The trace below is supported by
`outputs/investigation-priority-002/results.json`, `rows.current-circuit.r8`, and
the [action runtime contract](ACTION_COMMAND_RUNTIME.md).

1. `runtime.initial_state` retains canonical birth and separate judgment/session
   state. `runtime.advance` validates/deduplicates an envelope and dispatches the
   selected experiment handler; it is not a common four-domain cognitive pipeline.
2. `action_runtime.handle` checks explicit command authority and pinned setup.
   `action_policy.prepare` supplies eight registered historical outcomes, scoped
   instrument roots and an advertised exact correction capability. c9 has payload
   true, shield check1 false, check2 true. Instrument registration certifies report
   identity, not truth. Initial support is inconsistent and the prediction abstains.
3. `action_policy.select` invokes `consistent_attention.select`. Each candidate
   gets a NEW scratch `consistent-frame` birth, not the individual's evolving node
   activations. Direct values are Novelty=1, Salience=1, Theory=.5, Fact=coverage,
   Confidence=resolved, Clarity=inconsistent, Execution=0. Six authored edges run
   two synchronous passes at the existing gain .25. The score reduces exactly to
   `clamp(.25*(route_weight+.3125-coverage-resolved+inconsistent),0,1)`.
4. Preflight independently filters scope, active target/digest, permit, availability
   and already-resolved support. Positive question priority and action-ID ties select
   `correct-c9-a`; `request` records the pending choice and spends the one opportunity.
   The shared runtime correlates its emitted request and reply. Propagation cannot
   authorize the operation or certify its proposed replacement.
5. `action_policy.receive` delegates to `correction.correct`: exact authorization,
   active-head/coordinate checks and the independent reference accept check1=true.
   The old false record is preserved; the root advances to `replacement/c9-a`.
   Effective shield support becomes true/true under the authored equality relation.
6. `consistent_attention.predict` requires both questions resolved and payload true.
   `attention.decision` selects the historical shielded branch, whose signs are
   +1,+1,+1,−1. The existing counter gives +1/3 and threshold .2 yields success.
   Only afterward does the evaluator identify this as wrong. This is not a verified
   assertion that every shielded transmission succeeds. No final-outcome label
   enters selection or updates the frozen prior in this comparison.
7. Receipts, raw records, heads, request closure and traces persist in the checkpoint.
   `runtime_store.Store.commit/read` provides atomic replacement and checksum checks;
   source manifests restrict restart compatibility. Registered later contrary evidence
   can restore abstention while the historical request remains closed. That is
   already demonstrated in the sequential-correction study, without proving truth.

The causal chain contains useful coordination between ordinary modules. It does
not traverse Cultural or Philosophical nodes. Only seven leaf positions participate
in this circuit: two Experiential and five Psychological. Other seed nodes have
no declared edges or nonzero inputs in this path. `propagate_activation` explicitly
does not infer edges from genealogy or labels. Its numerical confidence/plasticity
fields are recorded but do not weight this formula. There is no hidden parent-to-
child route through which all forty nodes already influence the decision.

Clarity=inconsistency is a conflict-priority proxy, not validated interpretive
clarity. Confidence=resolved is support completeness/consistency, not calibrated
evidence strength or confidence in the branch's future outcome. Novelty and Salience
are fixed here, not history-derived appraisals. Intention, Phase, Synchronization,
Cultural and Philosophical activity should stay explicitly absent until a task
gives them a discriminable role; filling every slot would hide these limitations.

- [Seed contribution](../experiments/SEED_CONTRIBUTION_RESULTS.md): intact resolves
  4 questions versus 7 with the Clarity edge disabled; three hurts, no helps; final

  mapping, not every possible coordinated LMFT architecture.
- [Applicability](../experiments/CONTEXT_APPLICABILITY_RESULTS.md): remembered,
  independently calibrated hypotheses improve correct support decisions 192→352,
  zero wrong; 64 gains are on unseen contexts, 96 newly calibrated. Memory matches

  mechanism within its supplied family; it is not seed-mediated coordination.
- [Priority comparison](../experiments/INVESTIGATION_PRIORITY_RESULTS.md): current
  and target policies match on six executable cases. Conservative final-oriented
  selection saves r9's request but leaves one extra question unresolved. x2 may
  add a missing value without resolving its contradictory question. No universal
  utility score or always-investigate policy follows.
- r8 lacks a predictive distinction after valid coordinate repair. r10/c3 has
  agreeing fallible reports; existing corrections can expose their error, but the
  selector skips already-resolved support. The [audit assessment](CONSISTENT_SUPPORT_AUDIT_ASSESSMENT.md)
  found no matched evidence for learning when audits pay. Neither failure is fixed
  by activating more nodes or assigning a source a trust number.

Other implemented paths are also separate: `binding_runtime` persists learned
binding through the supervisor but bypasses the attention/propagation circuit;
the applicability engine is experiment-local. Shared checkpoint storage is not
evidence that their interpretations, constraints and action consequences interact.

## 4. Original proposed protocol — superseded by specified v2 and review addendum

**Goal:** establish that independently learned interpretation, current permission
and retained history jointly control an inspectable action disposition across
restart. Do not test a full seed theory or learn when to audit. The sensor bench
is a small synthetic setting, not a claimed realistic maintenance environment.
Defer repairs: current correction fixtures have incompatible coordinate-reference
assumptions, and inventing a repair service would enlarge this increment.

**Reversible default task:** a controller may issue a `release` disposition when
two scoped measurements support ready=1 under a calibrated interpretation; otherwise
it records `hold` with distinct not-ready/unknown/unauthorized reasons. One public
permission bit gates release. These are simulated dispositions only. The controlled
reference bit independently defines ready; a release when ready=0 is a failed release.
Holding when ready=1 is a foregone opportunity, not silently assigned moral failure.
Report both without combining them into utility. This default is a conformance
objective requiring two measurements, not proof that this sufficiency rule is optimal.

**Reuse before expansion:** retain the six wiring worlds, four calibration schedules,
two value flips and six-hypothesis family of the accepted applicability protocol.
Use its 56 support cases per stage, before/after the single follow-up, unchanged.
Pair each case with permission allowed/denied, independently of hidden wiring and
reference value. Thus 48 persistent histories, each with 112 support situations
and two permission states; these are repeated checks, not independent replications.
Keep existing missing/conflicting/duplicate/foreign cases. No case-ID rule.

**One coherent state/transaction record:** versioned calibration history and survivor
set; immutable scoped raw observations; current public permission; decision record
referencing the exact interpretation/evidence/permission versions; calibration
budget spent; eventual correlated consequence receipt. Freeze each disposition
before its delayed outcome is supplied. Each trajectory reloads after initial
calibration and after follow-up, then resumes the same history and costs.
Idempotent replay must neither respend the opportunity nor duplicate consequences.
This is a new experiment-local composition, not registration of a production mode.

**Public information:** context bits, raw observations, source/scope, explicitly
offered calibration slot and its cost, permission and the accumulated admitted
calibration history. Only accepted independent calibration constrains hypotheses.
Actual wiring, future reference values and held-out outcomes stay with the device/
evaluator until the appropriate post-choice boundary. Opaque history IDs carry no
world information. Delayed task outcomes are retained as consequence evidence,
not covert extra calibration or an expanded training budget. Learning a policy
from those outcomes is outside this first connection test.

**Costs:** one logical reference opportunity per history, even if unavailable;
count release/hold dispositions, denied attempts, records/checks and duplicate work
separately. No money, latency, institution trust weights or new node numbers.
All controllers have identical opportunities. Availability/cost is supplied by
the task, not inferred from which archived answer is wrong.

**Controls, frozen together:**

1. Coordinated primary: accepted applicability kernel feeds the current support
   view, public permission gate and persisted decision record.
2. Competent ordinary memory: the independent truth-table kernel receives the same
   information, policy, verification, costs and persistence. Also expose its direct
   decision path. Equal results are expected and count as useful engineering parity.
3. Frozen interpretation: same received observations and opportunity cost, but
   dispositions use the pre-follow-up interpretation snapshot. This isolates whether
   the new handoff actually uses learning, not merely logs it.
4. Stale-context intervention: a permission-revocation event between saved intent
   and disposition tests version linkage. The disconnected control retains the old
   intent; an identical external permission boundary rejects unauthorized execution
   in every condition. Count stale attempts, never actually bypass authority.

The last two are deliberately partial causal controls, not competent competitive
baselines. No seed propagation participates in this proposed connection; a direct
no-propagation controller is therefore the proper equivalent, not an all-zero
Execution control that disables action. Do not claim a propagation advantage.

**Falsification and acceptance:** pre-action logs must prove the disposition reads
the current persisted interpretation and permission, with no reference/outcome
leakage. Unknown context, empty family, ambiguity and raw contradiction must remain
distinguishable. Report action differences against frozen interpretation, unsafe
releases, foregone opportunities, remaining uncertainty, authority-denied attempts,
logical cost, exact memory parity and restart/replay invariance per context/stage.
If learning changes only displayed labels but never an eligible disposition, the
connection claim fails. If stale permission is honored, history/coordination safety
fails. If results require hidden IDs/labels, more information than memory, or a
new hand-tuned score, reject the comparison. If a competent simpler first-channel
diagnostic performs as well on truth/cost, report that too; it does not conform to
the two-measurement task gate, so distinguish task compliance from empirical utility.

Passing establishes a useful boundary: retained learned interpretation now governs
a permission-checked, consequence-linked decision rather than a detached support
report. It still contains authored gates, supplies no new cognitive interpretation
of seed labels and may add no predictive gain beyond the already accepted study.
That limitation is the reason to keep the increment small rather than introduce
a maintenance simulator and call ordinary programmed policies conceptual emergence.

## 6. Parent-review revision: distinguish a missing adapter from missing seed coordination

The factual architecture assessment is accepted. The original next-step recommendation
is withdrawn as the strongest immediate task. [Specified protocol v2](COORDINATION_HANDOFF_PROTOCOL_V2_PROPOSED.md)
now freezes identities/typed events, the actual synthetic device and verifier pipe,
trusted permission issuer, transaction versions/rejection, actual restart boundaries,
evaluator receipts for holds and releases, receipt isolation, all five comparators,
10,752 main dispositions per controller and separate intervention denominators.
It is retained for precision and possible later use, **not recommended for execution
now**. Evaluation receipts are not physical consequences or consequence-driven learning.

### What the larger handoff would actually add

`binding_runtime.newborn/handle/predict/check_runtime` already connects admitted
training and selected acquisition to retained survivors, read-only decisions,
correlated requests/closures, one-shot budget, launch-manifest validation and restart.
`action_runtime.handle` plus `action_policy` already connect operator authority,
scoped actions, reference-checked replies, request budget, evidence lineage and
current-versus-historical support. Both use the same atomic supervisor/store.
Their accepted tests already cover much of the proposed handoff's alleged novelty.

The genuine missing interface is narrower: `context_applicability_engine.evaluate`
returns experiment-local mapping votes and supported predictions from strict integer
bit/context/calibration records. No production consumer accepts that result as the
versioned interpretation of an action observation. `consistent_attention.normalized`
instead trusts `case['relation']` equal/complement; `binding_runtime` accepts its
separate neutral-binding schema. Neither may safely receive a cast, fabricated
relation or copied survivor list from a different contract.

An implementation of v2 would add a bench-identity/typed-event wrapper, interpretation
snapshot references, intent/disposition versions, a separate evaluator-receipt ledger
and an adapting driver/worker. It would NOT add any Cultural/Philosophical causal path,
learn when audits pay, or make outcomes revise interpretation. Estimated minimal
scope: two experiment Python files (one transition/worker module about 200–300 lines,
one driver about 150–250), plus roughly 200–300 lines of tests: approximately
550–850 new lines, zero production files. These are planning estimates, not measured
implementation. Most invariants repeat existing accepted integrations; the actual
applicability-to-view adapter would be only a small fraction. That cost is not
justified as a response to the owner's concern about seed coordination.

### More substantive smallest connection: interpretive scope -> Contemplation agenda

**Revised single recommendation:** operationalize one context-dependent interpretive
scope distinction and its bounded influence on deliberation, before adding another
transaction framework. Prepare one experiment-local connection contract for the
existing applicability state to determine the kind of a permitted question. Do not
implement it under this documentation task or assign new seed activation numbers.

A concrete engineering default is to distinguish these public, evidence-derived
situations rather than conflate them as raw sensor conflict:

| Public state at the target context | Provisional deliberative meaning | Bounded candidate behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nonempty hypotheses agree on interpretation | The present interpretation is supported here | Use existing support gate; no interpretation query merely because context changed |
| Nonempty hypotheses disagree here | Interpretation is underdetermined here | Request only an already offered, unspent independent calibration capable of distinguishing relevant interpretations; otherwise retain uncertainty |
| No hypothesis fits admitted calibration | The supplied interpretive family is unsupported | Suspend predictions and record an assumption-review issue; no forced singleton, automatic reset or unauthorized family expansion |
| A raw part contains both values | Evidence conflict survives every normalization | Retain that conflict separately; calibration cannot be advertised as repairing it |

This is a proposed deliberation contract, not a claim that those rows ARE the meanings
of philosophical nodes. A plausible context-dependent philosophical contribution
would concern when to maintain a shared interpretive assumption across episodes
versus mark its scope insufficient. Continuity/Discreteness may participate in that
interpretation later, alongside other distinctions; they are neither fixed synonyms
for stable/unstable nor binary flags to populate automatically. No numeric semantic
mapping, graph edge or decision authority is established by this suggestion.

The actual missing connection is from remembered, scoped interpretation status to
Contemplation's question KIND and applicability, beyond the current Clarity proxy
`C=inconsistent` and Confidence proxy `Q=resolved`. A direct controller with all the
same status/history/available actions is mandatory; expected parity would show an
ordinary useful mechanism, not a fuzzy-tree advantage. A raw-support-only controller
is a deliberately partial causal intervention, not the competitive baseline. Keep
current numerical equations and protected seed untouched unless separately approved.

### Honest prerequisite chain and earliest empirical step

1. **Already evidenced:** independent calibration can separate target-context
   consensus, ambiguity and unsupported-family status, without using agreement as
   truth. Changing public context does not erase generalized hypotheses. Do not
   repeat this accepted result and rename it philosophical reasoning.
2. **Testable now with existing inputs, but not yet evaluated here:** contrast the
   SAME raw support under different admissible calibration histories/target contexts;
   enumerate only the existing offered calibration slot and remaining budget. Does
   interpretive status change a legitimate question/deferral while retaining raw
   conflicts? Use the existing device/reference and no new worlds or audit cases.
   Where no offered action can help, the correct record is unresolved, not a
   manufactured question. This tests an evidence-to-deliberation connection, not
   another durable receipt system. Freeze exact offering, target and information-gain
   semantics before implementation; none may depend on the observed future reply.
3. **Required before a philosophical-contribution claim:** identify a versioned,
   revisable interpretive assumption with explicit supporting/counterexample records;
   specify which bounded agenda decision it may affect and what evidence can revise
   it. Its authority cannot come from the node label. Define an intervention removing
   that contribution while leaving evidence, operators, budget and ordinary memory
   intact. If behavior differs only in descriptions or labels, there is no contribution.
4. **Later only:** establish that a scoped representation involving a philosophical
   distinction improves a useful decision, retention or transfer compared with the
   equally informed direct mechanism, without importing a hidden label. Null results
   and memory equivalence remain valid. Mere activation of a previously unused leaf

5. **Faithful verbalization comes after grounding/application:** context evidence

   traceable measurable effect; only then should language express that specific
   effect with its scope, alternatives and uncertainty. Recognizable phrasing or a

   Rendering a known distinction is a separate problem from learning which
   distinction applies. A later deterministic explanation could first expose the

   authorization and fidelity checks, not permission to add an ungrounded meaning.
   The parent's Agency/Existence example of presence versus capacity-to-act remains
   illustrative only: it is neither canonical semantics nor an approved mapping.
   Philosophical context or nuance may develop without immediately forcing a fixed
   phrase for every activation pattern. No language-model work is proposed here.

## 7. Final refinement: the missing linguistic interpretation capability

The owner's latest concern identifies a more fundamental gap than the parked
handoff. Current binding/applicability mechanisms select among supplied executable
hypotheses over numbers/bits. They do not discover new linguistic membership
classifications, acquire word senses, or develop contextual working definitions.
Choosing an authored hypothesis is learning within a supplied family; attaching
an English label to its result would not turn it into linguistic learning. Existing
local-model proposal/teacher paths do not establish this capability either.

**Final single recommendation:** first freeze a tiny evidence/assessment contract
for a revisable, context-scoped candidate interpretation linked to existing seed
nodes. This supersedes the agenda-connection recommendation as the first prerequisite;
that connection becomes a possible downstream consumer after grounding exists.
The appropriate immediate deliverable is a contract and independently separable
example/evaluation set, not an ontology, new node inventory or another numeric fixture.

Reversible engineering default: one ordinary ambiguous expression, two observed
contexts of use, and a bounded set of candidate interpretations, with an explicit
none-of-the-above/insufficient-context outcome. The parent's contrasting 'order'
examples (predictable structure versus imposed coordination) are illustrations,
not accepted senses, seed definitions or gold labels. Select actual source passages
before finalizing candidate boundaries. Link a candidate to existing node IDs as a
proposed contextual membership, not as a redefinition of a founding node.

Minimum candidate record: stable non-founding interpretation ID/version; expression
and context scope; concise proposed working meaning and node-link rationale;
source/author provenance; supporting passage IDs; counterexamples and contrastive
uses; applicability limits; status proposed/accepted-within-scope/rejected/abstained;
revision and supersession history. Keep raw passages immutable. Do not invent fuzzy
membership strengths yet: a categorical proposed link with uncertainty and an explicit
abstention is a more honest first boundary than unsupported decimal precision.

A candidate is not accepted because a model coined persuasive wording. Separate
candidate generation, evidence-based revision and evaluation. Freeze a small training
set of independently sourced uses and contrastive counterexamples before generation;
then freeze held-out examples from different sources/topics and changed surface
wording. Include the same word in different contexts, paraphrases without the word,
misleading lexical overlap, genuinely underspecified uses and uses outside all
candidate scopes. Split by source/example family rather than random paraphrases.
No held-out labels or passages may revise the candidate before its evaluation.
Record errors, coverage, abstention and preservation of previously supported contexts;
do not collapse them into a convenient overall success score.

The smallest behavioral probe is selecting or rejecting a context-scoped working
interpretation for a new passage, with traceable supporting and contradictory
examples. Compare against a fixed authored glossary and ordinary example memory
with exactly the same training passages, generation access and evaluation rules.
If a runtime model assists, also compare that same model with ordinary structured
memory under matched prompts/budget; distinguish persistent unassisted application
from fresh model inference on each test. A frozen-candidate control can isolate
whether evidence-backed revision improves held-out application. If there is no
independent way to judge the cases, call the work exploratory annotation, not a
validated learned classification. Wording recognition alone is not semantic transfer.

Human source selection/annotation or a separately authorized, bounded model-assisted
proposal step is probably needed to obtain meaningful linguistic examples and
candidate meanings. No such corpus, reliable adjudication rule or executable
applicability representation is currently established by the numeric studies. A
fully hand-authored two-sense classifier would test our rules, not new meaning
acquisition. We should explicitly choose and disclose which parts are supplied,
proposed by a model, revised by the persistent system and independently evaluated.
The present task authorizes none of those model calls, corpus collection, teacher
expansion or implementation. Parent review can scope that next step separately.

This is a narrow prerequisite toward philosophical contextual interpretation,
not a claim that 'Order' or any other seed label has acquired a canonical dictionary
meaning. Grounding/application must precede its influence on Contemplation and then
faithful verbalization. Preserve the earlier capability assessments as history;
stop this documentation task here rather than expanding the proposed architecture.


Representation limit: dictionary concepts must not be forced into abstract binary
extremes or made to fit by brittle exact weights across many seed nodes. The six
philosophical leaves do not establish a representation of full lexical meaning.
Candidate classifications may require overlapping, context-specific associations,
with examples, relationships, applicability conditions and exceptions preserved in
revisable records outside the protected founding inventory. Unknown or unrepresented
meaning must remain an explicit possible outcome. This is not authorization to
expand the seed or tune weights. Any future weighted proposal must justify stability
under reasonable perturbations and contextual variation, rather than depend on
precise fitting to authored examples.


Owner clarification of intended geometry: the Philosophical structure is valued

Continuity/Discreteness, distinct from the intended Cultural top-down and
Experiential bottom-up structures. This is a landscape for contextual framing in
