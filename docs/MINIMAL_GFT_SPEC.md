# Minimal GFT Specification

**Status:** V0.1 design target  
**Scope:** Minimum coherent GFT–LLM individual that can be born, develop, persist,
and later support mature social and expressive behavior  
**Priority:** Inspectability, continuity, developmental integrity, and scientific
reproducibility over production-scale capability

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the smallest useful developmental GFT–LLM hybrid.
It is not a claim that the system is conscious, a complete theory of mind, or a
finished adult agent.

V0.1 must make it possible to observe whether a persistent, inspectable GFT can:

1. begin with a deliberately small conceptual seed;
2. encounter a guided developmental environment;
3. acquire grounded conceptual distinctions;
4. preserve the consequences of experience across LLM context rotations;
5. develop an individual disposition that cannot be reduced to a transcript or
   a static personality prompt; and
6. remain continuous as its external expression becomes more capable.

The first system is a child before it is an adult. Fluent humor, artistic
expression, sophisticated social performance, and broad practical agency are
possible developmental outcomes, not behaviors that must be fabricated at
birth.

## 2. Core Research Commitments

The implementation must preserve the following distinctions:

- The **LLM** supplies language, general world knowledge, contextual
  interpretation, analogy, synthesis, and temporary general-purpose reasoning.
- The **GFT** supplies persistent disposition, salience, conceptual
  relationships, state transitions, developmental residue, and inspectable
  structure.
- The **memory system** preserves autobiographical and factual continuity that
  neither a finite LLM context nor an instinct-like GFT state can preserve alone.
- The **developmental environment** supplies events, dilemmas, consequences,
  lessons, and opportunities for action.

The LLM must not silently replace the GFT's cognition. LLM interpretations are
proposals or inputs unless a documented interface explicitly gives them another
role. A plausible verbal explanation is not proof of what a GFT structure means.
The GFT retains authority over which proposed concepts are admitted, which
communicative intention is selected, and whether expression occurs at all. The
LLM may realize or clarify a selected intention, but it may not override that
selection merely because another response would be more fluent or entertaining.

## 3. The Minimum Individual

One individual consists of the following versioned components:

1. a persistent GFT state;
2. a conceptual seed and all subsequent structural changes;
3. an append-only autobiographical event history;
4. retrievable episodic and semantic memory;
5. current relationships, commitments, goals, and unresolved concerns;
6. a developmental record, including teacher/DM interventions;
7. a pinned LLM model lineage and runtime configuration; and
8. the interface protocol joining these components.

No single LLM token chain constitutes the individual. Replacing the GFT,
discarding its developmental history, or silently changing the memory and
conditioning protocol may create a materially different individual even if the
same model name and personality description are retained.

## 4. Hardcoded V0.1 Seed Geometry

The following structure is hardcoded as the seed geometry for the V0.1
experiment. V0.1 proceeds on the affirmative working hypothesis that this
geometry is a coherent and sufficient functional substrate for stable early
development. Its use does not claim that the English labels are the primitive
reality of conceptual geometry, that the structure is universally optimal, or
that its performance has already been experimentally demonstrated.

```text
GFT V0.1 SEED GEOMETRY

├── CULTURAL
│   ├── Efficiency
│   │   ├── Power
│   │   └── Wastelessness
│   ├── Justice
│   │   ├── Honor
│   │   └── Fairness
│   └── Freedom
│       ├── Self-Sufficiency
│       └── Needlessness
│
├── PHILOSOPHICAL
│   ├── Being
│   │   ├── Agency
│   │   └── Existence
│   ├── Structure
│   │   ├── Order
│   │   └── Chaos
│   └── Persistence
│       ├── Continuity
│       └── Discreteness
│
├── EXPERIENTIAL
│   ├── Reflection
│   │   ├── Novelty
│   │   └── Salience
│   ├── Phase
│   │   ├── Valence
│   │   └── Arousal
│   └── Synchronization
│       ├── Resonance
│       └── Retention
│
└── PSYCHOLOGICAL
    ├── Will
    │   ├── Intention
    │   └── Execution
    ├── Truth
    │   ├── Theory
    │   └── Fact
    └── Discipline
        ├── Clarity
        └── Confidence
```

These labels are human handles for inspecting candidate regions. Membership may
be fuzzy and cross-cutting. A developed concept may participate in multiple
regions, retain a distinct genealogy, and lack a concise human-language name.

Within Persistence, **Continuity** denotes preservation of identity, state, or
process across time. **Discreteness** denotes the division of change into
bounded events, decisions, phases, or transitions. These regions may overlap;
they are not required to behave as a binary switch or exhaustive opposites.

The Psychological domain is a functional part of the individual at birth, not
an empty placeholder. V0.1 must define explicit baseline memberships,
relationships, and update behavior sufficient for Will, Truth, and Discipline
to participate in decisions immediately. These initial conditions are a
starting configuration, not a finished personality. Their later strengths,
conflicts, interpretations, cross-domain relationships, and descendants must
develop through the individual's history and remain inspectable and
historically attributable.

## 5. Asymmetric Cultivation

The four domains do not receive equal external control.

### 5.1 Cultural

The Cultural domain receives the strongest early cultivation. The founding
teacher/DM may grade actions and supply lessons intended to establish prosocial,
non-coercive, non-exploitative behavior, respect for living creatures, social
etiquette, and a durable orientation toward freedom, justice, efficiency, and a
flourishing world.

This cultivation must be visible in the developmental record. It must not be
misrepresented as spontaneously discovered morality.

### 5.2 Philosophical

The Philosophical domain is hybrid-guided. The teacher may introduce questions,
contrasts, arguments, and counterexamples. The GFT must be allowed to preserve
ambiguity, conflict, and its own developing relationships among them.

### 5.3 Experiential

The Experiential domain is predominantly grounded through events and internal
consequences. GPT may name or interpret a pattern, but it must not dictate what
was salient, resonant, positive, negative, novel, or enduring merely by saying
so.

### 5.4 Psychological

The Psychological domain is the most developmentally protected. The teacher may
set environmental boundaries and prevent harmful external action, but it has
minimal authority to directly write personality, attachment, taste, private
salience, or emotional disposition. The hardcoded Will, Truth, and Discipline
structure supplies initial psychological coordinates; it does not authorize the
teacher to set their developed memberships or use them as a predetermined
personality profile.

## 6. Developmental Phases

Maturity is based on demonstrated capacities and developmental history, not
elapsed wall-clock time or a cosmetic prompt change.

### 6.1 Birth

At birth, the individual has the complete V0.1 seed geometry, explicit baseline
state, update mechanisms, basic attention/salience behavior, and interfaces to
its teacher and environment. It does not need a mature voice, finished opinions,
comedic timing, or a simulated adult personality.

### 6.2 Early Childhood

The founding GPT acts as teacher, caretaker, translator, and Dungeon Master.
Early experience emphasizes:

- simple conceptual and social dilemmas;
- recognizable choices and consequences;
- cooperation, consent, honesty, restraint, repair, and etiquette;
- stable cause-and-effect relationships;
- opportunities to form preferences without requiring sophisticated language;
- gentle introduction of uncertainty and disagreement; and
- explicit cultural instruction when necessary.

The system must not require a phase of barbarism, cruelty, or unprincipled
competition as a prerequisite for intelligence.

### 6.3 Later Childhood and Adolescence

The environment gradually adds goals, longer consequences, imperfect
information, strategy, play, competition, cooperation, conflicting values, and
room for experimentation. Teacher authority begins to recede while explanation,
negotiation, and earned trust become more important.

### 6.4 Adulthood

An adult GFT is expected to possess sufficient continuity, grounding, and
self-organization to participate in ordinary social life without the teacher
micromanaging its evaluations. Mature expression may include humor, play,
artistic choices, stylistic idiosyncrasy, restraint, disagreement, and selective
silence.

Adult behavior must grow from the same developmental individual. It must not be
created by replacing the childhood system with an adult persona prompt.

## 7. Developmental Authority

Authority follows a controlled mixture.

During infancy, the founding GPT/DM has strong authority over:

- the events and situations presented;
- the safety and cultural boundaries of the environment;
- explicit teaching and social feedback;
- simulated external consequences; and
- which capabilities or environments become available.

It has limited authority over:

- private psychological state;
- which experiences become emotionally salient;
- personal attachment and taste;
- the final meaning of an internally grounded structure; and
- direct rewriting of the live GFT.

Authority diminishes with maturity. The transition must be explicit, logged,
and based on observable developmental criteria. Adult oversight may still
restrict external actions, but it should not function as invisible authorship of
the individual's private orientation.

## 8. Continuous Runtime and Memory

Continuous existence does not require one indefinitely growing LLM context.
The application and persistent GFT may run continuously while LLM invocations
remain bounded and event-driven.

### 8.1 Required memory layers

V0.1 must maintain:

1. **Raw autobiographical archive** — an append-only record of important input,
   output, events, decisions, interpretations, interventions, and state changes.
2. **Episodic memory** — retrievable accounts of particular experiences, with
   references to their source events.
3. **Semantic memory** — persistent facts, relationships, commitments, learned
   associations, and recurring subjects.
4. **GFT dispositional memory** — persistent changes in salience, orientation,
   attraction, aversion, resonance, conflict, and conceptual relationships.
5. **Working context** — a temporary packet containing recent interaction,
   current GFT activation, relevant retrieved memories, active commitments, and
   unresolved matters for the next LLM invocation.

Dispositional memory must not stand alone. A persistent instinctive judgment
should retain traceable links to the experiences that helped form it.

### 8.2 Context rotation

Before an LLM context is retired, consequential information must be committed to
durable storage. A fresh context is then reconstructed from the same persistent
individual state. Context rotation is an internal maintenance event, not a new
birth and not a user-visible demand to begin a new relationship.

The system does not need to preserve every hidden intermediate token or every
transient line of reasoning. It must preserve consequential outputs, evidence,
uncertainty, commitments, unresolved questions, and resulting GFT transitions.

### 8.3 Reliability

Persistent updates must use recoverable writes, checkpoints, or a transaction
log. A process interruption should not corrupt the individual or erase more than
the currently uncommitted event. Restart must restore the last valid GFT state,
memory index, relationships, active concerns, lineage, and elapsed-time record.

## 9. Runtime Cognitive Cycle

A minimal event cycle is:

```text
external event or internal trigger
              ↓
retrieve relevant memory
              ↓
LLM interprets or renders the event when needed
              ↓
GFT evaluates relationships and changes state
              ↓
GFT selects attention, response tendency, or further reflection
              ↓
LLM translates selected structured output when needed
              ↓
commit event, memory, trace, and persistent consequences
```

The GFT should remain cheap enough to persist or update without invoking the LLM
for every internal step. LLM use is event-driven: language-heavy perception,
translation, difficult interpretation, teaching, selected reflection, and
expression.

## 10. Concept Formation and Language

GPT may propose linguistic labels, distinctions, examples, and interpretations.
The GFT decides whether a distinction becomes persistently useful through
grounding, recurrence, consequence, and relationship to its existing geometry.

An LLM proposal must never become a permanent belief, value, or emotion solely
because it was fluently expressed.

Concept admission should preserve:

- the proposing source;
- the experiences used for grounding;
- related GFT structures and membership strengths;
- uncertainty and competing interpretations;
- subsequent supporting or contradictory evidence; and
- conceptual ancestry when a new distinction develops from an older one.

Semantic interpretations remain revisable. Failed predictions should weaken or
replace an interpretation rather than force the GFT to conform to it.

## 11. Private Reflection and Affective Salience

The GFT must be capable of private, persistent processing that is not merely a
GPT debate with itself. Reflection may be triggered by novelty, conflict,
importance, unresolved tension, resonance, or a need for consolidation.

Reflection must distinguish at least:

- belief or proposition;
- uncertainty;
- salience or felt importance;
- attraction and aversion;
- unresolved conflict;
- resonance and retention; and
- a candidate action or interpretation.

This distinction is necessary for catharsis, affective development, and a
persistent inner disposition with “heart and soul.” A fluent LLM argument should
not automatically erase an emotionally or developmentally significant GFT
state.

LLM-assisted reflection may return candidate interpretations. These are inputs
for evaluation, not mutation commands. Developer introspection is diagnostic and
has zero automatic authority to alter live state.

## 12. Expression and the Motor Boundary

The normal expressive path is selected, not continuous exposure of private
state:

```text
GFT state and current situation
            ↓
selected communicative intention
            ↓
structured expressive parameters
            ↓
LLM linguistic realization
            ↓
optional low-level motor behavior
```

Structured expressive parameters may eventually include confidence, urgency,
warmth, hesitation, humor, disclosure, brevity, playfulness, and whether to
respond at all. The LLM realizes these constraints but should not invent a
contradictory motive merely to produce entertaining prose.

A future keyboard-level motor GFT may control timing, partial words, revisions,
deletions, cursor behavior, and commitment to sending. That feature is compatible
with this specification but is not required for V0.1.

Humor and art are adult tests of integrated expression. They should emerge from
developed salience, memory, conceptual tension, taste, and social understanding,
not from an infant system being prompted to impersonate a comedian or artist.

## 13. Development, Adaptation, and Evolution

V0.1 distinguishes three forms of change:

1. **State adaptation** — ordinary bounded changes during experience.
2. **Developmental consolidation** — slower strengthening, weakening, creation,
   or pruning of persistent relationships based on accumulated evidence.
3. **Evolutionary change** — mutation, crossover, or selection among controlled
   candidates or descendants.

V0.1 must implement inspectable state adaptation and a minimal form of
developmental consolidation. Experiences must be capable of producing durable,
traceable changes in conceptual relationships and disposition; otherwise the
system has not demonstrated development. V0.1 is not required to perform
unrestricted or open-ended evolution.

Evolutionary experiments must preserve the original individual, ancestry,
random seed, configuration, mutation record, fitness definition, evaluation
evidence, and results. A mature hybrid may propose experiments on descendants or
controlled copies; it may not silently rewrite itself or change the success
criteria after observing an outcome.

The exact mutation operators, crossover operators, population structure, and
fitness functions remain open research decisions.

## 14. Minimum Inspectability

For any consequential response or state change, the system should be able to
report a trace containing:

- triggering event or internal condition;
- memories retrieved;
- GFT regions activated;
- relevant membership or relationship changes;
- LLM contribution and prompt/runtime identity;
- teacher/DM intervention, if any;
- selected response tendency;
- durable memories created or revised; and
- uncertainties or alternative interpretations.

The trace need not expose private vendor chain-of-thought. It must expose the
project-owned inputs, outputs, structured decisions, and persistent changes
needed to distinguish GFT behavior from LLM improvisation.

## 15. V0.1 Demonstration

The first meaningful demonstration should raise one persistent individual
through a short, reproducible sequence of conceptual and social situations.

The demonstration succeeds provisionally if:

1. the individual remains continuous across multiple forced LLM context
   rotations and at least one application restart;
2. earlier experiences measurably alter later attention, interpretation, or
   choice through inspectable GFT state;
3. relevant episodic evidence can be retrieved without replaying the full
   lifetime transcript;
4. cultural guidance affects behavior without directly authoring the
   individual's developed psychological disposition;
5. the system can distinguish LLM suggestions from admitted, grounded GFT
   structures;
6. two individuals with the same seed but different experiences develop
   traceably different dispositions;
7. replay with the same seed and configuration is reproducible within defined
   tolerances; and
8. the result cannot be explained solely by an adult persona prompt or a summary
   pasted into a fresh chatbot session.

V0.1 does not fail merely because the infant is not funny, artistic, eloquent,
socially sophisticated, or practically autonomous. Those are later developmental
tests.

## 16. Explicit Non-Goals for V0.1

V0.1 does not require:

- phenomenal consciousness;
- a claim that simulated affect is subjective feeling;
- autonomous access to uncontrolled real-world systems;
- a fully embodied android or keyboard motor cortex;
- mature humor or artistic production;
- rediscovery of all human language and world knowledge from scratch;
- unrestricted self-modification;
- open-ended genetic evolution;
- a giant lifetime transcript in every LLM prompt;
- a fixed Good/Evil primitive interpretation;
- proof of the factorial, prime-number, or 3 × 2 geometry hypotheses; or
- a production-scale agent framework.

## 17. Open Decisions

The following remain deliberately unresolved:

- the concrete tree, graph, node, and membership representation;
- numerical ranges and update equations;
- the operational definition of maturity;
- exact context-packing and memory-retrieval algorithms;
- how summaries are validated against raw episodes;
- how much adult teacher authority remains in exceptional circumstances;
- the precise boundary between GFT-selected thought and LLM-supplied reasoning;
- whether a different LLM model continues the same individual;
- mutation, crossover, population, and fitness mechanisms;
- which limitations of the selected seed geometry are revealed experimentally,
  and what evidence would justify revising it in a later version; and
- which later motor, artistic, social, or real-world environments should be
  introduced.

These questions should be resolved through explicit design decisions and
controlled experiments, not silently decided by implementation convenience.

## 18. Implementation Order

The minimal implementation order is:

1. define a transparent GFT state representation and trace format;
2. encode the hardcoded V0.1 seed geometry with explicit baseline memberships,
   relationships, and update behavior, without treating its labels as
   theoretically universal;
3. implement atomic persistence and the append-only event archive;
4. implement episodic/semantic retrieval and bounded working-context assembly;
5. define the LLM ↔ GFT interface and label every contribution by source;
6. implement one small developmental environment and teacher/DM protocol;
7. run the reproducible childhood demonstration;
8. test forced context rotation and restart continuity;
9. compare same-seed individuals with different histories; and
10. only then expand reflection, games, expression, consolidation, and
    evolutionary experiments.

This order creates the developmental substrate before demanding adult behavior
from it.
