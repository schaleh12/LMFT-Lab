# Evidence-learning handoff

## Current status — outcome repair completed

The [outcome-correction study](OUTCOME_CORRECTION.md) completed this handoff on 2026-10-11.
Its [results](../experiments/OUTCOME_CORRECTION_RESULTS.md) record 371 passing tests and
766/766 evaluated checks. Accepted replacements preserve ancestry, contribute once per
event, recompute the unchanged score, and survive restart with behavioral consequences.
Ordinary memory matches LMFT; no prediction improvement or propagation contribution was
demonstrated. Teacher integration remains unchanged and unused.

The smallest proposed next experiment is one independently verified contrary outcome
after correction and restart, with a preregistered expected score and a memory control.
Test that correction does not freeze the judgment against later eligible evidence. This
follow-up has not been implemented.

## Historical handoff from the teacher study

The following records the prior task boundary and motivation, before outcome repair was
implemented; its future-tense descriptions are historical.

The bounded [teacher briefing study](../experiments/TEACHER_BRIEFING_RESULTS.md) closes

### Then-next bounded task — now completed in the separate study

Correct one mistaken outcome sample and recompute the **unchanged** judgment equation from
one effective record per independent outcome root:

`score = sum(effective signs) / (effective independent sample count + 2)`

Use a fresh preregistered fixture. Preserve the mistaken original, correction provenance,
exact target digest, authorization, verification, acceptance reason and before/after state.
A replacement supersedes the original for scoring; it is not an additional sample.

The existing correction experiment only repairs scoped condition observations. Its
`heads`/`roots` ledger retains originals and replaces the effective view, but its target
eligibility excludes prior outcome samples. Existing `judgment.apply_validated` recomputes
the counter from its evidence mapping; it is not an outcome-correction API. The next task
must explicitly connect effective outcome roots to that same numerical calculation rather
than overwriting originals or incrementing the independent count.

### Original suggested minimum preregistration

- One revisable judgment, one clearly erroneous registered outcome and one independently
  verified authorized replacement. Fix evidence eligibility and target identity beforehand.
- A read-only probe whose response may change with the existing thresholds. For example,
  if supported by the new fixture: two positive/two negative effective samples score 0;
  correcting one negative to positive scores 2/6 = 1/3 with four independent roots. This
  is an illustrative arithmetic check, not newly accepted fixture evidence or a changed rule.
- Three matched conditions: enabled correction, correction disabled, and ordinary memory
  recomputing the same score. No teacher or model call is necessary.
- Verify immutable originals, causal score/probe change, evidence/reason retention, stale
  and unauthorized correction rejection, duplicate delivery without double counting,
  actual restart and atomic recovery. Invalidate any affected derived caches explicitly.
- Preserve both successful and negative results, record storage/time and compare effective
  sample counts with stored record counts. Do not credit ordinary ledger behavior to
  fuzzy propagation unless an independently tested causal contribution exists.

Keep the canonical seed, activation/propagation equations, judgment formula and thresholds,
existing revision/correction policies, migrations, historical studies and communication
boundary unchanged. Use a separately versioned outcome eligibility rule; do not silently
expand the historical scoped-observation fixture or migrate historical individuals.

### Original readiness and blockers

No teacher-model or hardware blocker is known for this deterministic next step. The missing
piece is explicit outcome-target eligibility and projection of effective outcome records into
the existing counter, with provenance and atomic persistence. That is the next implementation
scope, not a reason to delay for more teacher work. Freeze its concrete fixture and acceptance
criteria before running it. No outcome-correction implementation was added in this task.
