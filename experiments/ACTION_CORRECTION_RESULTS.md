# Action-aware missing evidence versus verified correction — results

The bounded external action policy avoided six non-resolving contradiction gathers
made by the unchanged original selector. It selected eligible corrections instead
of treating every unresolved question as a request for more evidence. Equally
equipped ordinary memory matched exact rankings, actions, correction acceptance
and final predictions. This is a benefit of the added action/eligibility policy in
these authored cases, not evidence of seed understanding or fuzzy superiority.

## Mechanism and comparison

[Harness](run_action_correction.py) applies an explicit external action adapter.
Missing required parts can be gathered from an available scoped service. Inconsistent
support can request an advertised permitted operation on a current exact target.
Metadata eligibility never certifies replacement truth: unchanged `correction.correct`
rechecks exact authorization, active target/digest/coordinate and the existing
independent replacement reference. Unpermitted, wrong-scope, stale, irrelevant and
already-resolved offers cannot silently rewrite evidence. Missing and inconsistent
support are separate properties; both action types can be eligible for one question.

Eligible actions use unchanged question circuit scores, positive-score gating and
lexicographic action-ID ties. Memory uses the exact scalar reduction with identical
support, offers, eligibility, replies, receiver and budget. Existing Clarity ablation
disables only its existing edge. Filtered gather-only shares the new eligibility
filter but excludes corrections. Original control calls unchanged circuit.select
directly, without action filtering, then gathers all required parts of its selected
question. All conditions have the same authored services/capabilities; the original
selector does not use correction or availability metadata to choose its action.

Original full-question replies may append corroborating roots at already covered
coordinates; they never supersede conflicting originals or outvote them. Stored
records/root counts do not establish statistically independent observations.
These scoped authored services are synthetic references, not real-world truth
discovery or identity authentication. Exact operation permits are public capabilities.

## Results and denominators

Twelve scenarios per condition, two relevant questions each, at most one shared
logical request per scenario. Newly resolved counts false-to-true COMPLETE CONSISTENT
support. A non-resolving request does not achieve that transition for its selected
question, including unavailable or rejected replies. It is distinct from decision
correctness and from a request that fails to enable a final decision.

| Condition | Requests (correction/gather) | Non-resolving | Unresolved contradiction gathers | Newly resolved | Decisions/correct | Coverage/accuracy |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Action-aware circuit | 6 (4/2) | 2 | 0 | 4 | 6/4 | 50% / 66.7% |
| Equally equipped scalar memory | 6 (4/2) | 2 | 0 | 4 | 6/4 | 50% / 66.7% |
| Existing Clarity ablation | 6 (3/3) | 2 | 0 | 4 | 6/4 | 50% / 66.7% |
| Filtered gather-only | 3 (0/3) | 1 | 0 | 2 | 4/3 | 33.3% / 75% |
| Unchanged original selector | 9 (0/9) | 8 | 6 | 1 | 4/3 | 33.3% / 75% |

No requests target already-resolved questions. Full/memory/ablated enable three new
decisions; filtered/original enable one. Their accuracy covers different subsets;
the added repair in r8 still predicts incorrectly, so higher coverage does not imply
higher accuracy. r10's initially sufficient evidence also retains an incorrect
prediction. No outcome/criterion changed to make repair appear successful.

In r1/r8, verified correction removes a conflict and enables success. r4's permitted
wrong-value replacement passes authorization/eligibility but fails verification.
r2 gathers a missing part after preparation and resolves; r12 gathering adds
coverage but exposes conflict and still abstains. Original r11 spends a request on
unavailable gathering, returns no observation and performs no receiver check.
r9 intact chooses correction while ablation/filtered gather missing payload; each
resolves one question and still abstains. Its Clarity ranking effect has no final
decision benefit. All per-scenario traces and retained wrong outcomes are archived.
