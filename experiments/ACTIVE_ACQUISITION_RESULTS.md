## Cost counts and remaining limit

Every REQUESTING episode has: request_count=1, request_cost_units=1,
admitted_acquisitions=1, scripted service observations=1, acquisition RECEIVER
verification executions=2 (one false-label rejection plus one valid label).
Other conditions have zero for all acquisition counts. Every individual has
four admitted training records but FIVE reserved training verifier executions,
because a false registered t1 allegation is checked and rejected first; validation
has four admitted records/four verifier executions. Cost is instrumented in receipts,
including rejection, without scientific-state mutation. Malformed/unrelated/duplicate
replies cause no simulation checks. False-label receipts retain the allegation and
reason, never count as genuine hypothesis contradictions.

Across twenty main individuals: eight requests/units, eight admitted acquisitions,
eight actual scripted service observations, sixteen acquisition checks; eighty
training admissions/one hundred training checks and eighty validation admissions/
eighty validation checks. These are scripted receiver costs, not timing estimates
or a bound on arbitrary future traffic.

**Remaining concrete limitation:** request/admission budget does NOT cap receiver
verification attempts. Repeated eligible false replies with FRESH IDs while pending
can trigger further simulator checks and receipt growth. They cannot resolve falsely,
reset budget or issue another request, but total receiver work is unbounded by this
episode policy. The reported two acquisition checks belong to the frozen script,
not a universal maximum. No attempt cap/timeout was specified or implemented, and
this task does not expand scope to add one. That boundary needs a separately frozen
policy before extension. Accepted-set revision after later evidence also remains
