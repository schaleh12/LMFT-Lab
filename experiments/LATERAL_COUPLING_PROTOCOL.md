# Lateral Ethical-Ontological Coupling Protocol (Phase 1)

## 1. Research Question & Purpose
Can the 6 Cultural leaves (Ethics/Governance) and the 6 Philosophical leaves (Metaphysics/Ontology) be dynamically cross-coupled through a principled lateral resonance matrix such that moral conscience and existential reality mutually check and balance each other without mutating or expanding the frozen 40-node founding seed (Rule D001)?

## 2. Invariants & Guardrails
- **Rule D001 (Frozen Founding Seed):** The canonical 40 founding nodes in `birth.py` must remain completely invariant. Lateral coupling operates strictly upon dial activations within the existing 12 Cultural and Philosophical leaves.
- **Rule D004 (Matched Ordinary-Memory Baseline):** Evaluate against a matched structured-memory comparator running the identical matrix transformations; require 100% byte-for-byte state digest parity.
- **Neutral Quiescence Invariant:** When all leaves are at neutral baseline ($0.50$), all lateral deltas must evaluate strictly to $0.00$, introducing zero artificial drift.
- **Continuous Bound Enforcement:** All coupled dial activations must be strictly bounded within $[0.0, 1.0]$.
- **Zero Runtime LLM Calls:** All coupling transformations must be computed deterministically offline.

## 3. Benchmark Dataset Structure (6 Scenarios)
1. `tyranny_risk_power_overreach`: High Cultural Power ($0.90$) induces compensatory Philosophical Order to prevent tyranny.
2. `existential_volatility_chaos`: High Philosophical Chaos ($0.95$) triggers moral ballast on Justice/Honor and Freedom/Self-Sufficiency.
3. `societal_equity_continuity`: High Cultural Fairness ($0.85$) bolsters Being Existence and Persistence Continuity.
4. `ascetic_sovereignty`: High Freedom Needlessness ($0.90$) bolsters serene Existence and Agency.
5. `atomic_frugality`: High Efficiency Wastelessness ($0.85$) reinforces Persistence Discreteness.
6. `neutral_equilibrium_quiescence`: All coordinates at $0.50$ baseline verify zero drift.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria
- 100% adherence to $[0.0, 1.0]$ bounds across all coupled leaves.
- Strict $0.00$ delta on neutral quiescence scenario.
- Observable compensatory stabilization in non-neutral scenarios (governance balance and moral ballast).
- Exact byte-for-byte evaluation digest parity against ordinary memory comparator.
